Pixel check

Each icon is rasterised at its true size (1 device pixel per grid unit at 20 px, 0.8 at 16 px) then enlarged 8 times with no smoothing. The faint grid marks single pixels.

20 px
16 px (scaled from the same SVG)

Strokes sit on whole units, so at 20 px the 2 px lines land on full pixels. At 16 px the 2 unit stroke becomes 1.6 px and shows a little anti-aliasing, which is expected when one drawing serves both sizes.
